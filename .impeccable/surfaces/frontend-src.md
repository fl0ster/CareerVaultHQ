# Surface: Pica app (frontend/src, whole SPA)

Mode: Operate. The person is the single self-hosting owner, working through the jobs inbox, applications, interviews and their career record. They come back often and use it calmly between searches.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A magpie at golden hour. The app is a flat, saturated field of dusk violet (dark) or dawn lilac (light). Sun yellow marks what to press or what is new, and iridescent feather blue marks where you are. It refuses the grey admin card grid: no shadows, no glossy gradients, depth only from tonal steps.

OWN-WORLD: Dark "Sunset" has an ink-violet ground, plum-violet surfaces, lavender text, a sun-yellow action pill with ink text, a cobalt-to-violet feather highlight and a peach glow for warmth. Light "Sunrise" has a pale lilac-peach ground, near-white violet-tinted surfaces, indigo ink, a deeper marigold action and a cobalt feather. Headings and big numbers are set in heavy Bricolage Grotesque, and the UI text in Plus Jakarta Sans. Pills for actions and generous curved rectangles for containers. Hairlines are used only where tone alone cannot separate things. There are no forest or scenery motifs.

STORY: The person sees what is new, what is waiting and what is next at a glance, then acts with one yellow pill. The magpie shows up where something shiny arrives (new jobs) and where work runs (worker status).

FIRST VIEWPORT: A violet sidebar holds the profile card, the nav and a theme switch. On the Jobs entry, the new-jobs count sits in a yellow pill with the magpie head peeking over it. The top bar holds breadcrumbs and the worker magpie. The main area opens with a heavy grotesque page heading, the stat row with big display numbers on tonal tiles, and then the work areas. The primary action is the yellow pill in each page's header or toolbar.

FORM: An operate-mode app shell with the world limited to type, palette, density and one signature move (the magpie beside new-item counters). seed: none (pinned): "Use the colour palette ... The dark mode should be forrest at sunset, with purples, highlight (the magpie feather colour) and yellow highlight, the light mode should be the same forrest at sunrise ... without shadows or too much gradients ... the new jobs notification counter could have the magpie head alongside."

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
